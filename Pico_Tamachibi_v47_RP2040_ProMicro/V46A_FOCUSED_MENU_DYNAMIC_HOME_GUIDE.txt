PICO TAMACHIBI v46a FOCUSED MENU + DYNAMIC HOME
================================================

MENU CONTROLS
-------------
D0: previous item
D1: open selected item
D2: next item
Hold D2: return from supported submenus

Only the selected item is shown as a large icon. The right panel explains the
selection and shows its position. The small side-icon previews were removed.

HOME LAYOUT
-----------
The Pet Room changes according to who actually lives there:

SOLO
- Main Tamagotchi is centered.
- Former family space becomes a reading nook.
- No spouse-shaped empty panel is reserved.

WITH SPOUSE
- Main Tamagotchi moves left.
- Spouse receives a separate protected pad.
- Companion is placed between them without covering either sprite.

WITH CHILDREN
- Child uses the far-right family pad.
- Main Tamagotchi shifts left enough to keep both visible.

WITH SPOUSE AND CHILDREN
- Pet, companion, spouse and child each use separate horizontal zones.

WORLD CONTROL
-------------
The GP26 potentiometer still exclusively selects:
- Low: Tamagotchi
- Middle: Home
- High: City

Buttons do not switch between those three world scales.
