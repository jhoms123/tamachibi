PICO TAMACHIBI v45 LIFE-STAGE GAME CATALOG
============================================

ACCESS RULES
------------
- Sleeping is the only state that blocks games.
- At home, Games opens a choice between Arcade Games and Life-Stage Games.
- During school or work, Games opens the current stage's five schedule games.
- Stage games can also be practiced outside scheduled hours.
- Playing never restores energy and therefore cannot replace night sleep.
- Every completed stage game costs 1 energy.
- Games played during the stage's scheduled activity window receive a bonus.

BABY: FIVE EARLY-DEVELOPMENT GAMES
----------------------------------
1. Toy Catch
   Move under falling toys. Builds bonding, attention, and gentle coordination.
2. Crawl Around
   Move around soft obstacles. Builds movement skill without using sleep recovery.
3. Clap Along
   Press Select when the moving marker enters the target zone.
4. Peekaboo
   Memorize and repeat left/right patterns.
5. Shape Sort
   Sort circles and squares into matching bins.

Baby routine bonus: 9:00-11:00 AM and 2:00-4:00 PM.

CHILD: FIVE SCHOOL-DAY GAMES
-----------------------------
1. Pencil Catch
   Catch falling classroom supplies.
2. Hallway Dodge
   Avoid backpacks and hallway obstacles.
3. Recess Jump
   Time jumps inside the safe target zone.
4. Spelling Steps
   Memorize and repeat left/right spelling patterns.
5. Lunch Sort
   Sort lunch items into the correct category.

School bonus: weekdays from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
Successful school-time play can increase intelligence, discipline, and Study Stars.

TEEN: FIVE ADVANCED SCHOOL GAMES
--------------------------------
1. Note Catch
   Catch falling notes and study pages.
2. Locker Rush
   Dodge lockers and crowded hallway obstacles.
3. Band Timing
   Hit the rhythm target at the correct moment.
4. Quiz Pattern
   Memorize longer left/right answer sequences.
5. Group Project
   Sort project materials into the correct side.

School bonus: weekdays from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
Teen games develop intelligence, fitness, kindness, discipline, and school confidence.

ADULT: FIVE WORKPLACE GAMES
----------------------------
1. Supply Catch
   Catch falling office and job supplies.
2. Coworker Dodge
   Avoid rude or distracting coworkers and workplace hazards.
3. Meeting Timing
   Speak or act when the timing marker enters the target zone.
4. Inbox Order
   Memorize the correct sequence for messages and tasks.
5. Commute Sort
   Sort commute and work decisions into the correct path.

Work bonus: weekdays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM when the adult has a career.
Work-time wins provide extra career XP, reliability reputation, and Gotchi Points.

ELDER: FIVE ACTIVE-AGING GAMES
-------------------------------
1. Herb Basket
   Catch useful herbs and keepsakes.
2. Garden Stroll
   Avoid garden obstacles during a gentle walk.
3. Gentle Stretch
   Time stretches safely inside the target zone.
4. Story Order
   Remember and repeat the order of a family story.
5. Tea Sort
   Sort tea and table items into their correct places.

Elder routine bonus: 8:00-11:00 AM and 3:00-5:00 PM.
Elder games develop fitness, wisdom, kindness, bonding, and family mentoring.

CONTROLS
--------
Menus:
- D0: previous
- D1: select/play
- D2: next
- Hold D2: exit

Catch/Dodge:
- Hold or tap D0/D2 to move.

Timing:
- D1 stops the marker.

Memory/Sort:
- D0 chooses left.
- D2 chooses right.
- Hold D2 for about three seconds to leave the game.
