PICO TAMACHIBI v45 CHANGE NOTES
================================

MAIN REQUEST
------------
School and work no longer remove gameplay. The pet remains unavailable for
ordinary care while following its real schedule, but the Games action stays
available at every awake life stage.

ADDED
-----
- 25 life-stage games: five each for Baby, Child, Teen, Adult, and Elder.
- A Game Type screen at home: Arcade Games or Life-Stage Games.
- Direct access to life-stage games while the pet is at school or work.
- Schedule bonuses for school, work, baby routine, and elder routine hours.
- High-contrast game objects and protected player silhouettes.
- Stage-specific rewards connected to intelligence, fitness, kindness,
  discipline, Study Stars, career XP, reputation, coins, bonding, and trust.

SLEEP AND ENERGY
----------------
- Sleeping still blocks every game.
- Games do not wake the pet.
- Games never restore energy.
- Every completed life-stage game removes 1 energy.
- If bedtime arrives during gameplay, scheduled sleep begins after the game.
- School and work games do not replace the required night-sleep recovery.

SCHEDULE LOCK CHANGE
--------------------
The away-time action gate now permits M_GAME while retaining the lock on food,
cleaning, medicine, travel, shopping, and other actions that should require the
pet to be home.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
------------------
No save structure, EEPROM address, checksum, or profile format was changed.
Existing v44 and compatible older saves continue to load.
