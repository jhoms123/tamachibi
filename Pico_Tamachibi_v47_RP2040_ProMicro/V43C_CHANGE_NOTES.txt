PICO TAMACHIBI v43c - AGE-COHERENT DIALOGUE FIX
=================================================

ROOT CAUSES FIXED
1. The separate Growing Together engine could open during the baby stage.
2. Its baby milestone was incorrectly linked to the Starting School scenario.
3. Sixty-five Parent Dialogue topics shared only twenty-one response sets.
   Several sets were unrelated to the prompt, such as a lonely new classmate
   receiving apology/end-friendship choices.
4. The 240 Life Events reused generic category actions instead of actions tied
   to the exact event.
5. A pending conversation saved before an evolution could survive into a life
   stage for which it was not written.
6. The basic Talk menu used adult-like actions such as Ask Secret and Give
   Advice even while the character was a baby.

AGE RULES
- Egg: no conversation.
- Baby: short babble, attachment, food, sleep, mess, imitation, safe affection,
  and first companion contact only. Growing Together is locked.
- Child: school, sharing, honesty, simple responsibility, play, safety, and
  child-level family questions.
- Teen: identity, peer pressure, privacy, rivalry, grades, future planning,
  embarrassment, boundaries, and increased independence.
- Adult: work, partnership, parenting, money, community responsibility,
  burnout, and long-term goals.
- Elder: memory, regret, mentoring, legacy, family history, and life change.

RESPONSE COHERENCE
- 65 Parent Dialogue topics now have 65 exact response sets.
- That is 195 exact choice labels and 195 exact remembered replies.
- Questions are tied to the exact three choices shown next.
- 240 Life Events now have 240 exact prompt summaries and 480 exact choices.
- Growing Together retains 27 feature-specific question/choice families, but
  now has hard stage gates and scenario-level stage filters.

OLD SAVE PROTECTION
- Invalid pending Parent Dialogue topics are discarded as soon as the stage is
  checked, then replaced with a valid topic.
- Invalid pending Growing Together features/scenarios are also discarded and
  reselected.
- A baby can never display a saved school, career, marriage, co-parenting, or
  elder narrative.

READABILITY
- Parent Dialogue and Growing Together remain word-wrapped and scrollable.
- Text stays open until D1 is pressed.
- Life Events now open in a full-width scrollable reader before choices.
- Long Life Event choices wrap over two lines instead of being cut off.
- The confirmation page also stays open until D1.

BASIC TALK MENU
During the baby stage, the menu now uses baby-appropriate interactions:
- Make silly sound
- Cheer baby
- Ask sleepy dream
- Play peekaboo
- Teach gentle hands
- Baby temperament
- Look at fish
Growing Together displays Unlocks at child rather than opening adult text.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
No save structure, checksum, EEPROM address, or profile layout changed.
Existing v43/v43a/v43b saves remain compatible.
