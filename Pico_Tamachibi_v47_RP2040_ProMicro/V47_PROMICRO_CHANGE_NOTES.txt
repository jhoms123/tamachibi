V47 RP2040 PROMICRO CHANGE NOTES
================================

1. Renamed sketch and folder for the RP2040 ProMicro target.
2. Changed board documentation from Raspberry Pi Pico to Generic RP2040.
3. Reserved GP17 for the onboard RGB LED and drive it LOW at startup.
4. Kept the supplied dual-OLED mapping: GP4/5 and GP6/7.
5. Kept buttons GP10/11/12, buzzer GP15 and dial GP26.
6. Reserved GP0-3 for SD and GP20-22 for future I2S audio.
7. Moved the optional disabled battery monitor enable from GP22 to GP29.
8. Changed the splash label to v47 PROMICRO.
9. Added ProMicro-specific Arduino IDE and upload instructions.
10. No gameplay, save layout, dialogue, growth, scheduling or visual logic was removed.
