PICO TAMACHIBI v44 - REAL-DAY CLOCK GUIDE
==========================================

PURPOSE
-------
v44 connects the Tamagotchi's routine to the player's actual day. The pet no
longer recovers through repeatable daytime quick rests. It sleeps, wakes, attends
school, and works according to the weekday and time entered at startup.

WHY THE CLOCK IS ENTERED AFTER POWER-ON
---------------------------------------
The standard RP2040/Pico cannot remember the current time while completely
unpowered. The saved game remembers the last known epoch, but the player must
provide the current weekday and clock time after a full power cycle. While the
board remains powered, the software clock advances normally even when the OLED
is off.

BOOT CLOCK FLOW
---------------
The editor has four persistent screens:
  Day -> Hour -> Minute -> AM/PM
D0 decreases, D2 increases, and D1 confirms. Minutes use five-minute steps to
make entry fast with three buttons. The saved timestamp anchors the week so an
overnight shutdown moves forward to the following day rather than backward.

SCHEDULE TABLE
--------------
Stage   Bedtime    Wake       Weekday daytime obligation
Baby    7:00 PM    7:00 AM    Home
Child   8:00 PM    7:00 AM    School 8:00 AM-3:00 PM
Teen    10:00 PM   7:00 AM    School 8:00 AM-3:00 PM
Adult   11:00 PM   6:00 AM    Work 9:00 AM-5:00 PM if employed
Elder   9:00 PM    6:00 AM    Home

Saturday and Sunday do not schedule school or work. An adult without a selected
career stays home.

SLEEP RULES
-----------
1. Bedtime is automatic.
2. Wake time is automatic.
3. The Sleep/Light action cannot begin a daytime rest.
4. Selecting it during daytime displays the next bedtime.
5. Selecting it at night displays the wake time; it cannot skip sleep.
6. Normal sleep restores one energy point per in-game hour.
7. Four or more hours of real sleep guarantees at least 9/10 energy on waking.
8. Powering off overnight and entering the morning time safely credits the
   crossed sleep window without applying artificial powered-off neglect.

SCHOOL AND WORK
---------------
The pet is unavailable for ordinary care actions while scheduled away. The pet
remains visible in a high-contrast classroom or office scene, with furniture
kept outside the protected character zone.

School effects:
- Intelligence growth
- Discipline growth
- School location shown until 3:00 PM

Work effects:
- Career experience
- Modest pay
- Work location shown until 5:00 PM

Away-time protection:
- No impossible care calls
- No poop generation while away
- A noon meal provides limited fullness
- Night sleep remains required; school/work never grants sleep energy

CLOCK SCREENS
-------------
Settings > Set real clock reopens the editor.
Status includes a dedicated clock page showing:
- Weekday and time
- Current location
- Bedtime and wake time
- Weekday or weekend schedule

POWER-OFF LIMITATION
--------------------
Without an external RTC, a manual weekday/time entry cannot prove how many whole
weeks passed if the Pico was off for more than seven days. v44 therefore uses the
nearest forward matching weekday and safely credits only schedule/sleep recovery.
The optional DS3231 mode remains the accurate solution for long powered-off
periods.
