PICO TAMACHIBI v47 CHANGE NOTES
================================

- Added a second 128x32 SSD1306 status OLED.
- Added a dedicated Wire1 bus on GP6 SDA and GP7 SCL.
- Both OLEDs may use address 0x3C because they are on separate controllers.
- Added live food, fun, energy, cleanliness, health, and poop bars.
- Added slow blinking for attention and fast blinking for urgent needs.
- Added special School, Work, Sleep, Sitter, Egg, and Memory Star screens.
- The secondary display stays active when the main OLED times out.
- Main 128x64 gameplay, buttons, saves, and GP26 potentiometer view switching
  are unchanged.
