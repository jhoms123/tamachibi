PICO TAMACHIBI v46a CHANGE NOTES
================================

DYNAMIC HOME OCCUPANCY
----------------------
- The Pet Room no longer reserves a permanent empty spouse position.
- When no spouse or children are present, the Tamagotchi is centered and the
  right side becomes a bookshelf, lamp, books and reading cushion.
- When a spouse or children are present, the room automatically switches to a
  family layout and moves furniture to the walls.
- Pet, spouse, child and companion positions reflow so the actors do not share
  the same protected sprite pad.
- The partner sprite is only drawn when a real partner has sufficient bond to
  live at home.

FOCUSED MENU
------------
- Removed the miniature previous/next icon cards.
- Main and category menus now show exactly one large selected icon.
- A separate information panel shows the title, plain-language purpose,
  availability and position in the menu.
- D0 and D2 chevrons remain as navigation cues, but no adjacent items are shown
  as misleading miniature choices.
- The old row of tiny page dots was replaced with a simple progress track.

UNCHANGED
---------
- The potentiometer remains the only control for Tamagotchi / Home / City.
- No save structures, EEPROM addresses, checksums or profile formats changed.
- All v46 gameplay, dialogue, schedule, family, city, garden, aquarium and
  Paradise systems remain present.
