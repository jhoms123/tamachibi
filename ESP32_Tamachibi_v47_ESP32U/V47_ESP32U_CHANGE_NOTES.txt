Tamachibi v47 ESP32-WROOM-32U breakout port

- Copied the complete v47 game implementation into a separate ESP32 target folder.
- Replaced RP2040 EEPROM/clock wrappers with ESP32-compatible wrappers.
- Reassigned the two I2C buses, buttons, piezo, rotary encoder, SPI microSD, and I2S output pins for the classic ESP32-WROOM-32U breakout.
- Replaced Arduino-Pico buzzer PWM calls with a helper covering Arduino-ESP32 core 2.x and 3.x.
- Added a Music Player that indexes up to 32 MP3 tracks from /music, decodes them, and sends audio over I2S to a separate future audio board.
- Kept the separate SPI microSD adapter; the HW-261/VS1053 shield is not used.
- The rotary encoder cycles Pet/House/City views and controls music volume.
- Preserved the original Music rhythm mini-game and v47 game systems.
- Added PlatformIO configuration and hardware/card setup guides.
- The optional battery monitor remains disabled; the charger/boost hardware must be identified before its exact wiring can be confirmed.
